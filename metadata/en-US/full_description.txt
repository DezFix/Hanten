Hanten is a free and open source manga reader for Android. Fork of Kotatsu (KotatsuApp/Kotatsu) and Futon (AppFuton/Futon), package hanten.wre.app.

<strong>Main Features:</strong>

- Online manga catalogs (1200+ sources) via DezFix/hanten-parsers + Tachiyomi keiyoushi extensions
- Search by name, genres and filters
- Favorites with categories
- History, bookmarks, incognito
- Download and offline CBZ support
- Material You UI for phones/tablets/desktop
- Standard and Webtoon reader with gestures
- Updates feed, recommendations
- Tracking: Shikimori, AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu
- Password/fingerprint protection
- Auto-sync
- Android 6.0+
- Direct in-app updates from GitHub Releases (no store, no Hanten)
