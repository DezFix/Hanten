Hanten is a free and open source manga, manhwa and light novel reader for Android. No ads, no account.

<strong>Main features:</strong>

- Today screen: continue reading, new chapters, "Coming soon" (estimated date of the next chapter) and recent reads
- 130+ Russian and Ukrainian sources (Senkuro, Remanga, MangaLib, RanobeLib and more) plus other languages, via DezFix/hanten-parsers
- Tachiyomi keiyoushi extensions support
- Library with colored folders, Reading / Not started / Finished filters and progress on every cover
- Search by name, genres and filters; similar titles and alternative sources
- Manga reader (standard and webtoon, gestures) and novel reader with themes and background text-to-speech
- Chapter downloads for offline reading, CBZ archives
- New chapter notifications with smart updates
- Profile with reading level, titles, achievements and statistics
- Tracking: Shikimori, AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu
- Move from other apps: Mihon / Tachiyomi (.tachibk), Kotatsu and Futon backups
- History, bookmarks, incognito, password or fingerprint lock
- Themes, accent color, corner style, bottom bar order
- Direct in-app updates from GitHub Releases
- Android 6.0+

Hanten is a fork of Kotatsu and Futon. Thanks to their developers.
